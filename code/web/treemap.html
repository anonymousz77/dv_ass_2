Interactions

  • Click a block to zoom into it; click the breadcrumb or Zoom out to go back up.
  • Hover for the value, share of parent and share of the total.
  • Change the hierarchy to remodel the same table into a different tree, the size measure, the tiling (spatial partitioning) algorithm, or the period.
Each leaf is a sum over country × year × hazard-subtype records of the EM-DAT country profiles (2023 excluded as a partial year). Deaths, people affected and damage are only summed where reported; damage is CPI-adjusted to 2022 US$.
