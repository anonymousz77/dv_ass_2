DAS732 Programming Assignment 2 – InfoVis browser visualisations

Team Pythagoras · Lakshya Jain (BT2024044) · Kalpit (BT2024093) · Arnav (BT2024233)

Node-link diagram and layout comparison

167 countries linked by the similarity of their natural-disaster mix (EM-DAT, 1970–2022). Five NetworkX layouts (Fruchterman–Reingold, Kamada–Kawai, ForceAtlas2, spectral, circular) plus a live force simulation, with filtering, focus on a country, zoom and drag.

Task owner: Kalpit (BT2024093)

Parallel coordinates with edge bundling

12 impact and hazard-mix dimensions for 167 countries (or 622 country-decades). Brushing on several axes, axis reordering by drag, axis inversion, and cluster-based edge bundling with adjustable strength and smoothness.

Task owner: Lakshya Jain (BT2024044)

Zoomable treemap

The same EM-DAT table remodelled into four hierarchies (geography, hazard taxonomy, hazard × geography, time), sized by events, deaths, people affected or damage, with six tiling (spatial partitioning) algorithms.

Task owner: Lakshya Jain (BT2024044)

Open the pages directly from this folder in a recent browser (Chrome, Edge or Firefox). The data are embedded as JavaScript files in data/; D3 v7.9.0 is bundled in lib/, so the pages also work offline. Append ?static=1 to disable animations.
